# Survey on Video and Multimodal Generation
## TL;DR
- Recent advancements in video and multimodal generation focus on unifying modalities for improved performance, evident through architectures like Composable Diffusion and systems such as JavisGPT [1][2][3].
- Key methods in video generation have shifted towards integration with textual components, leveraging GANs and VAEs to enhance outcomes [4][5].
- Significant challenges persist in ensuring temporal consistency and ethical concerns, necessitating future iterations on model governance and safety [6][3].

## Background
Video and multimodal generation encompasses the intersection of visual, audio, and textual content creation. Its historical context reveals an evolution from isolated models towards integrators that unify multiple modalities—such as GANs adapting to video contexts and diffusion models enhancing fluidity [7][2]. The field has rapidly advanced with an increase in applications spanning entertainment, education, and assistive technologies.

## Theoretical Foundations of Video and Multimodal Generation
The core theoretical frameworks within video and multimodal generation include advanced multi-modal generative architectures, notably the introduction of diffusion models that iterate on prior methodologies based on GANs and Auto-Regressive models. The architectural review highlights various strengths and weaknesses across models and emphasizes the role of contextual integration in improving generative fidelity [1][7][2]. 

## Advancements in Video Generation Techniques
Recent methodologies show significant progress in integrating text with video outputs. The hybridization of GANs and VAEs presents marked advancements, particularly in frameworks that generate coherent video sequences from textual prompts. Systems such as Temporal GANs have emphasized the importance of maintaining semantic coherence, improving interaction between modalities [4][5][8]. Moreover, predictive learning combined with motion priors has exerted influence over the efficiency of latent representations [8][9].

## Multimodal Generation Approaches
Multimodal generation has offered novel frameworks for the synthesis and congruity of audio-visual content. Frameworks like FlowInOne and C3LLM pave the way for integrating various sensory inputs that boost the generative quality across tasks—standing as benchmarks in synchronization and semantic alignment [10][11]. The emergence of Any-to-Any generation gives rise to flexible model architectures allowing diverse outputs [12][13].

## Trends and Open Problems
Despite the progress, the literature acknowledges persistent challenges such as achieving spatiotemporal coherence and addressing ethical frameworks in model deployment. Multimodal generative systems still require thorough evaluations for safety and robustness against biases. The future of research points towards better understanding generative risks, enhancing transparency, and ensuring the ethical integrity of AI systems [6][3][10].
