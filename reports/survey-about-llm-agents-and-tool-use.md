# Survey on LLM Agents and Tool Use

## TL;DR
- Large Language Model (LLM) agents integrate complex reasoning, autonomous actions, and interaction with external tools, enhancing their capabilities beyond traditional LLMs [1].
- LLM agents display versatile applications across multiple domains, including healthcare, finance, and education, showing significant advancements in operational efficiency [2][3].
- Tool integration methods vary from manual to automated frameworks, with automated approaches significantly improving response accuracy and adaptability [4].
- Major challenges remain, including error accumulation, long-horizon task execution, and hallucination risks, necessitating enhanced evaluation frameworks and robust safety principles [5][6].

## Background
The emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs) has revolutionized artificial intelligence, providing powerful frameworks for various tasks involving human language. LLM agents extend these capabilities by integrating autonomous functionality, allowing them to reason about tasks, formulate plans, and interact with a range of tools and APIs. This comprehensive review explores key themes and developments in the field of LLM agents, their applications across industries, integration methodologies, challenges encountered, and directions for future research.

## Core Concepts and Definitions of LLM Agents
LLM agents are distinguished by their architecture, often consisting of an agent core, memory modules, and planning components, which collectively empower them to process information, retrieve data, and leverage tools effectively. This advanced structure enables LLMs to not only respond to user queries but also engage in complex workflows, dynamically switching between tasks and utilizing past interactions to inform future actions [1].

## Application Domains of LLM Agents and Tools
LLM agents find extensive applications in diverse sectors. In healthcare, these agents analyze patient data and assist in diagnostics, leading to improved efficiency and patient outcomes. Similarly, in finance, they aid in market analysis and automate interactions, greatly enhancing the decision-making processes. The utility also extends to education, where personalized learning experiences are facilitated. However, the deployment of LLMs raises challenges, including biases and ethical concerns, particularly related to data privacy [3][5].

## Comparative Analysis of Tool Integration Methods
A range of methodologies for integrating tools with LLMs has been explored, from traditional manual approaches to dynamic automated frameworks. The latter category exhibits notable performance improvements, making agents more adaptable and capable of handling more complex interaction scenarios. Examples include frameworks that allow LLMs to call APIs as needed, enhancing their operational effectiveness significantly [4] [7].

## Trends and Open Problems
The journey of LLM agents is marked by numerous challenges. These include error accumulation during task execution, difficulties in long-horizon planning, and the generation of inaccurate information (hallucinations), particularly detrimental in critical applications. Future developments must focus on establishing robust evaluation frameworks to continuously assess and enhance LLM capabilities while addressing inherent risks and fostering safer interactions with tools and technologies [5][8].
