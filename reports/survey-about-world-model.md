# Survey about World Model

## TL;DR
- World models serve as essential tools for both understanding the environment and predicting future dynamics, bridging various disciplines such as robotics and AI [1][2].
- Key theoretical foundations include modular designs that integrate stochastic processes and causal reasoning principles, enhancing the functionality of predictive models [3][4].
- The architectures utilized for world modeling have evolved, with modern approaches emphasizing interactivity and real-world applicability, while leveraging diverse techniques like generative models [5][6].
- Current challenges encompass unclear definitions, long-horizon predictions, and action conditioning, pointing toward the need for standardized frameworks and further research into dynamic environments [7][8].

## Background
World models pertain to comprehensive systems designed to represent, understand, and predict complex environments. Historically, these models distilled understanding from diverse avenues, from simple probabilistic frameworks to intricate neural network architectures, progressively defining their roles in advancing artificial intelligence and robotics [1]. Noteworthy contributions in foundational research have laid the groundwork for their evolution.

## Theoretical Foundations
The theoretical underpinnings of world models highlight two main functions: constructing representations and predicting future states. Recent surveys categorizing these models emphasize their diverse applications across fields like autonomous driving and robotics [4]. Methodologically, training approaches leveraging prediction errors and multimodal generative modeling provide insights into improving accuracy and scalability in various tasks [2][4].

## Architectures & Latent Dynamics
Architecturally, world models exhibit considerable diversity, from recurrent networks to neural fields. These implementations prioritize accurate predictions through enhanced comprehension of underlying environmental dynamics, showcasing advancements in both stochastic and deterministic modeling [5][6]. Recent findings reveal that interfacing generative frameworks with real-world data significantly improves performance across complex tasks [6].

## Applications & Practical Implications
World models are pivotal in applications ranging from robotics to cognitive science. They facilitate complex decision-making processes, enhancing capabilities in navigation and planning through structured learning approaches [2][3]. Recent literature connects robot learning directly to predictive representations, indicating significant progress and outlining future challenges for continuous learning in dynamic environments [5][6].

## Trends and Open Problems
As world modeling continues to advance, key obstacles remain, such as the need for unified definitions and methodologies, particularly regarding action conditioning and long-horizon predictions. Future directions point toward improving dynamism in world models, necessitating detailed exploration of interactive systems that can adapt to real-world conditions effectively [7][8].
